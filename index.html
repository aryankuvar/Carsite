The power chain

Power flows through the car in a fixed order. Understanding this chain is key to understanding how the parts work together.

Engine → Flywheel → Clutch / Torque converter → Gearbox → Driveshaft → Differential → Axles → Wheels

Never skip a step — each part passes power to the next. Click a system above to explore its components.
